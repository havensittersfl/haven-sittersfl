Night shifts & bedtime

FAQ

Your questions, answered

What areas do you serve?

Orlando and Winter Park, Florida.

How do I book?

Start with a free 15-minute intro call. After that, we match you with your sitter and schedule your first booking.

Will I have the same sitter every time?

Yes. We match each family with one sitter so your kids can build real trust.

How are sitters vetted?

Every sitter is interviewed, completes Safe Environment Training and signs our company oath before their first shift.
